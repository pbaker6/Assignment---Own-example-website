One of my favourite pieces of classical music is Pergolesi's Stabat Mater, performed here by Nathalie Stutzmann.

Watch Pergolesi - Stabat Mater on YouTube

I find the sorrowful melody incredibly moving and it is an excellent example of Baroque sacred music.

I also highly recommend the music of the a cappella group, Vocce8. They are known for their exquisite harmony and interpretation of both classical and contemporary pieces.

Watch Gregorio Allegri - Miserere on YouTube

Their rendition of Gregorio Allegri's Miserere is particularly beautiful. Allegri composed this piece in the 1630s for use in the Sistine Chapel, and its ethereal melody and high C-note have made it one of the most famous pieces of choral music ever written.
